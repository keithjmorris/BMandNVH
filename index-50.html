Parish Council meetings

Burnham Market Parish Council meets in the hall nine times a year, at 6:30pm. There are no meetings in August, December or February.

Everyone is welcome to attend, listen to the debate and speak during the public session. Agendas are published three clear days before each meeting.

A new charity for the hall

Hire the hall

The hall can be booked by the hour or the whole day. A table tennis table is available to hirers, and audio-visual equipment can be provided on request.

All bookings are subject to confirmation by the Village Hall Committee.

Find us

The hall stands on a triangle of land between Beacon Hill Road and the B1355 Creake Road in Burnham Market. The entrance is on Beacon Hill Road.

About the hall

The hall was built in the 1960s and is governed by a trust deed dated 25 August 1960. It is run by volunteer trustees for the benefit of Burnham Market, Norton and the surrounding villages.
